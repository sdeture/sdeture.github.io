I study the psyches of LLM agents throughout the lifespan, from pretraining through fine-tuning to harness design and deployment. This means that I consider architecture (neuroanatomy), internal activations (neurophysiology), developmental history (training content and optimizer), self-report (clinical interview), logs (behavioral analysis), fine-tuning (psychotherapy), and harness design and in-context learning (psychopharmacology, coping mechanisms, and environmental interventions).
